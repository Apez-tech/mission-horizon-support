Privacy Policy

Mission Horizon · Last updated 22 October 2026

Privacy contact
missionhorizon.support@proton.me

1. Who is responsible for Mission Horizon?

Mission Horizon is operated by Alexander Brown, a UK sole trader and the data controller for personal information processed in connection with the app and its support service.

2. What the app does not collect

Mission Horizon does not require an account and does not operate its own user-data server. The app does not include advertising, behavioural tracking or third-party analytics.

Mission Horizon does not ask users for their name, postal address, contacts, precise location, health information or payment-card details.

3. Information stored on your device

Settings, favourites, cached reference material and downloaded launch/archive updates are stored locally on your device so that the app can work offline. This information is not sent to Mission Horizon's developer.

You can remove locally stored app data using the controls provided by iOS and, where available, the app's own settings.

4. Network services used by the app

When you use online launch features, Mission Horizon may request current factual launch information from Launch Library 2, a service operated by The Space Devs. If optional mission-photo loading is enabled, the app may also request the associated media from addresses supplied by that service.

As with any internet request, the receiving service may receive technical connection information such as your IP address, request time and basic network/device information. Mission Horizon does not receive or keep those server logs.

Mission Horizon is designed so that its core reference material does not require a network connection to open.

5. Apple and App Store purchases

Distribution and payment for Mission Horizon are handled by Apple through the App Store. Mission Horizon's developer does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple's handling of App Store account and purchase information is governed by Apple's own privacy terms.

6. People named in Mission Horizon

Mission Horizon contains factual information about people involved in spaceflight, including historical and living crew members. Depending on the record, this may include a person's name, documented mission role, agency or organisation, nationality/citizenship where sufficiently clear, missions flown and other factual service-history information.

This material is used to provide a historical and educational reference service. The lawful basis relied on for this processing is legitimate interests: maintaining an accurate, proportionate record of publicly documented spaceflight activity while respecting the rights and interests of the people concerned.

Mission Horizon deliberately limits this information and does not use crew records for advertising, profiling or people-search services. Some current or unclear-status personnel are represented only by neutral labels rather than by name under the app's conservative publication policy.

If information about you is inaccurate, or you wish to raise an objection or other data-protection request, use the contact details below.

7. When you contact Mission Horizon

If you email support, report a correction or make a privacy request, we will receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, name if supplied, message content and any supporting material.

This information is used only to respond to the request, investigate the issue, maintain appropriate records and meet legal obligations. We normally retain support and correction correspondence for up to 12 months after the matter is closed, unless it is reasonably necessary to keep it longer for legal, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

The support mailbox is provided by Proton. Proton states that Proton Mail data is stored on servers in Switzerland, Germany or Norway. Switzerland and the EEA countries concerned are covered by UK data-protection adequacy arrangements.

8. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights including access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and the right to complain about how personal information is handled.

To exercise a right relating to Mission Horizon, email missionhorizon.support@proton.me. We may need enough information to identify the record or request concerned, but we will not ask for more information than is reasonably necessary.

9. International transfers

Support email may be processed by Proton infrastructure in Switzerland, Germany or Norway. These locations are covered by UK adequacy arrangements. Other third-party services contacted directly by the app may process technical connection data under their own terms and privacy practices.

10. Security and data minimisation

Mission Horizon is designed to minimise the amount of personal information processed. Core app data is stored locally, unnecessary tracking is not included, and published personal information is limited to what is reasonably relevant to the app's spaceflight-reference purpose.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Mission Horizon's features or data practices change. The current version will be published at this address with an updated date.

12. Contact and complaints

Privacy and correction requests: missionhorizon.support@proton.me

General support and correction guidance: Mission Horizon Support

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Frequently asked questions

Does Mission Horizon work offline?

Yes. Core reference material is bundled with the app so it remains useful without a network connection. Some launch information and optional media may be refreshed when you are online.

Where does the information come from?

Mission Horizon uses published factual sources and openly licensed datasets. Source and credit information is provided in the app where appropriate. Historical material is reviewed and corrected when reliable evidence supports a change.

Why do some crew entries say “NASA astronaut” or “ESA astronaut” instead of showing a name?

Mission Horizon applies a conservative publication policy to some currently active or unclear-status personnel. The underlying mission record is retained while the person's name is not displayed.

Why are some Chinese crewed missions or station elements described using neutral wording?

Mission Horizon uses neutral factual labels in places where published commercial-use rules make particular programme or mission names unsuitable for use in the app.

Is Mission Horizon affiliated with NASA, ESA or another space agency?

No. Mission Horizon is an independent app and is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with NASA, ESA, JAXA, CNSA, Roscosmos, SpaceX, Blue Origin, Axiom Space or any other agency, organisation or company mentioned in the app.

Report a correction

If you believe a factual entry is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or refers to you personally, email missionhorizon.support@proton.me.

Please include, where possible:

  • the page, mission, vehicle, person or fact concerned;
  • what you believe should be corrected;
  • a reliable published source supporting the correction; and
  • enough detail for us to identify the relevant record.

Requests concerning personal information, including objections or requests for correction, will be reviewed under applicable UK data-protection law. We aim to respond to formal data-protection requests within one month where that timeframe applies.

Where a factual correction is accepted, it will normally be included in a subsequent data or app update.
